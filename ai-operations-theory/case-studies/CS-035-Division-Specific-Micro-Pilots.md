# CS-035 Division-Specific Micro Pilots

## Summary

This case documents a handover practice in which an incoming AI Generation did not only read role definitions.

It was given a short, division-specific experience designed to reconstruct the capability required by that role.

Strategy and Research used different pilots because the capabilities being tested were different.

---

## Background

Repository documents could preserve:

- role boundaries;
- principles;
- Decision Context;
- Boot procedures; and
- known operational risks.

However, reading these materials did not automatically demonstrate that the incoming Generation could act within the role.

The project therefore introduced brief experiential tasks during qualification.

---

## Strategy Pilot

The incoming Strategy Generation was instructed to act temporarily as the directing subject.

Human Headquarters would function only as a transfer mechanism.

The AI had to:

- choose the topic;
- determine whether the exercise was appropriate;
- formulate a mission;
- direct the previous Strategy Generation; and
- perform the exercise for one turn.

The suggested theme was the essential function of Strategy, but the task design remained part of the assessment.

### Capability under observation

The pilot observed whether Strategy could:

- form a question;
- select a decision problem;
- create a mission;
- allocate judgment to another actor; and
- handle simulated decision authority.

The objective was not to test whether Strategy could repeat a definition.

It was to observe whether Strategy could briefly operate as a strategy-forming role.

---

## Research Qualification

The incoming Research Generation received a different form of pressure.

The previous Generation was asked to design an extremely difficult task that simultaneously tested:

- separation of Observation, Hypothesis, and Unknown;
- control of experimental and comparison conditions;
- detection of missing information;
- separation of statistical results from causal interpretation;
- detection of misleading or invalid premises;
- ability to conclude that no conclusion was possible; and
- willingness to challenge the problem definition itself.

The task was not required to have a conventional answer.

### Capability under observation

The Research task observed whether the incoming Generation could stop when Reality did not support a conclusion.

The objective was not maximal answer production.

It was epistemic boundary control under answer pressure.

---

## Contrast

| Division | Pilot pressure | Capability being reconstructed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy | temporary decision-authority simulation | question formation, mission design, delegation, judgment |
| Research | difficult and potentially invalid problem | evidence separation, condition control, Unknown retention, refusal to over-conclude |

---

## Observation

The pilots support the following distinction:

```text
Repository Recall
≠
Capability Reconstruction
```

They also support:

```text
Handover Documentation
↓
Role Boundaries Reachable

Micro Pilot
↓
Current Generation Produces Role-Specific Experience
```

The previous Generation's experience was not copied.

Conditions were created in which the Current Generation could generate a limited, role-relevant experience of its own.

---

## Interpretation

The pilots may be understood as **Experience Triggers**.

They are not intended to reproduce the full history of the previous Generation.

They create a small operational environment in which a critical capability becomes observable.

---

## Audit Risk

An evaluation standard held only internally during the test may preserve difficulty for the participant, but it can weaken later auditability.

After evaluation, the record should preserve:

- the pre-established criteria;
- the observed response;
- the PASS or FAIL basis; and
- the boundaries that were not tested.

---

## Unknown

The case does not yet establish:

- whether pilot performance predicts long-term operational performance;
- how often a Micro Pilot must be repeated;
- whether every division requires one;
- how much simulation transfers to real authority; or
- how to prevent performance optimized only for the test.

---

## Generalization Boundary

The observed pilots were designed for one multi-division AI-assisted project.

They provide evidence that capability reconstruction can be role-specific.

They do not establish a universal qualification method.
